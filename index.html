Ivy House

A Quiet Place to Gather,
Stay and Slow Down

Tucked away in the quiet green pockets of Pune, Ivy House is a private home designed for slow living and thoughtful gatherings. Across different moments of the week, the house opens its doors in different ways:

  • A relaxed overnight stay to unwind and recharge
  • A daytime garden setting for small celebrations and creative gatherings
  • A seasonal long-table lunch experience coming soon

Ivy House is not a commercial venue. It is a home that welcomes small groups who value calm spaces, good food, and unhurried time.

Ivy House is best suited for guests looking for a relaxed, slow-paced stay rather than a party venue.

The Experience

A private stay
in green surroundings

Ivy House offers a private overnight stay designed for slowing down, reconnecting, and spending time together in a calm, green setting just outside the city.

Whether it's a small family getaway, a quiet celebration, or a relaxed weekend with friends, the house provides space to breathe, unwind, and enjoy the outdoors.

Ivy House is best suited for guests looking for a relaxed, slow-paced stay rather than a party venue.

House Guidelines

A home, not a venue

Ivy House is a private home in a residential setting. We host guests who appreciate calm spaces and respectful gatherings.

Guest Numbers

Maximum overnight capacity is 12 guests.

Music & Noise

Moderate music is fine during the day, but loud music is not supported.

Pool Use

The People Behind The House

About Ivy House

Our Story

A home opened
with intention

We have greatly enjoyed the experience of developing this farm house over the last 20 years. We along with our extended family and friends have always had a great time - be it for a short day stay or a long weekend. We have always offered it to friends, family and other like-minded groups of families who have enjoyed this experience. We hope that you too enjoy staying at the Ivy House.

We look forward to welcoming you — and we ask that you read and follow the guidelines we share so that everyone can enjoy a comfortable and safe stay.
